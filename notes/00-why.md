# Why?

Just recently my uncle died after years of a fight with prostate cancer. I remember how my aunt shared a photo of medical results of my uncle to our family WhatsApp group with some words, describing what it meant.

Now while reading this challenge and trying to decide what I should do, I again remembered about this photo. I was wondering what would an AI say, if we'd show them PSA results of my uncle.

> Would we be able to trust their explanation as we could with a doctor?

I didn't try. Do I regret it or not? I don't know. Also, I'm not sure this is the right moment to ask my family for these documents.

So I want to try to answer this question without my uncle's results. My plan is to try some small open weight model on some PSA reports. How it understands those? Especially if it doesn't have full medical history of patient.

I think that it might give a comforting answer, but without all the data. **This is just a hypothesis right now.** I want to check, if the tests confirm this hypothesis.
